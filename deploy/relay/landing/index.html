Why Enclave

One browser, many sealed lives.

Every profile is built to look like one ordinary computer, and to never cross into another.

Sealed profiles

Each profile is one container with a device fingerprint generated once and never changed. Cookies, storage and identity stay inside it.

Its own proxy, never leaks

Traffic only ever leaves through the profile's own proxy. If the proxy fails, the profile fails closed.

Continue on your phone

Pick up the exact session on your phone: same tabs, same logins, same IP. The browser keeps running on the PC.

Consistent by design

Time zone, language and location follow the proxy, so a profile never contradicts itself.

Local and private

Everything runs on your machine. No accounts, no analytics, nothing phoned home.

How it works

Three steps, then it just runs.

01

Create a profile

Give it a proxy. Enclave builds a fixed, believable device identity and checks the proxy before anything starts.

02

Browse sealed

The profile runs in its own container. Nothing crosses to another profile, and traffic only leaves through its proxy.

03

Carry it to your phone

Link your phone and continue the same live session anywhere. The PC keeps the browser running; the phone is a screen.
