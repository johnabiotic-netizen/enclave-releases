Enclave

Every profile is its
own sealed world.

A private browser for Windows. Each profile runs in its own sealed container, with its own fixed device fingerprint and its own proxy, and carries on to your phone in the exact same session.

Download for Windows All versions

Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit. Free beta. The installer isn't signed yet, so Windows may show a SmartScreen warning the first time.

Sealed profiles

Each profile is one container with a fixed device fingerprint, generated once and never changed. Nothing bleeds between profiles.

Its own proxy, never leaks

A profile's traffic only ever leaves through its own proxy. If the proxy fails, the profile fails closed, never your real connection.

Continue on your phone

Start on your PC and pick it up on your phone: same tabs, same logins, same IP, same fingerprint. The browser keeps running on the PC; the phone is just a screen.

Consistent by design

Time zone, language and location follow the proxy, and the device details match a real machine, so a profile looks like one ordinary computer.

Private beta. No accounts, no tracking, no analytics. You bring your own proxies.
