Why Enclave

One browser, many sealed lives.

Every profile is a world of its own, built to look like one ordinary computer and to never cross over into another.

Sealed profiles

Each profile is one container with a fixed device fingerprint, generated once and never changed. Nothing bleeds between profiles.

Its own proxy, never leaks

A profile's traffic only ever leaves through its own proxy. If the proxy fails, the profile fails closed, never your real connection.

Continue on your phone

Start on your PC and pick it up on your phone: same tabs, same logins, same IP, same fingerprint. The browser keeps running on the PC.

Consistent by design

Time zone, language and location follow the proxy, and the device details match a real machine, so a profile looks like one ordinary computer.

How it works

Three steps, then it just runs.

STEP 01

Create a profile

Give it a proxy. Enclave builds a fixed, believable device identity to go with it and checks the proxy before anything starts.

STEP 02

Browse sealed

The profile runs in its own container. Nothing crosses to another profile, and traffic only ever leaves through its proxy.

STEP 03

Carry it to your phone

Link your phone and pick up the exact same session on the go. The browser keeps running on your PC; the phone is just a screen.

The non-negotiables

Built so a profile can't betray you.

  • Fails closedIf the proxy drops, the profile stops. Your real connection is never used as a fallback.
  • Fixed fingerprintA profile's device identity is generated once at creation and never regenerated on launch.
  • Local and privateEverything runs on your own PC. No accounts, no tracking, no analytics.
